How trades are scored

A signal is confirmed at the daily close and entered at the next trading session's open. The target and stop are checked from entry onward; when both occur in one candle, the stop wins the tie. Only completed {{ summary.get("holding_days", holding_days) }}-session opportunities are counted, and returns include {{ summary.get("round_trip_cost_pct", round_trip_cost_pct) }}% round-trip costs.

The indicator's Minervini and quality scores are informational rankings, not entry alerts in the supplied Pine script, so they are not silently added as trade rules here.

Important: this uses today's Kite-listed NSE universe. Delisted stocks are unavailable, creating survivorship bias. Results are an event study without portfolio capital or simultaneous-position limits, not a tradable equity curve.
